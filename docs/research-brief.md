## 3. CRITIQUE

### GOOD — preserve
- **The nature palette.** Moss + Sun is genuinely distinctive in an enterprise-AI market saturated with indigo/violet gradients. It is under-exploited on the current site.
- **"Enterprise AI is won in production."** A sharp, ownable, defensible point of view. Most competitors sell capability; this sells *survival past the pilot*.
- **The integrator / deployer / infrastructure / transformation** four-role framing is clear and honest.
- **Real proof exists**: hard numbers and a roster of recognisable Nordic enterprises.
- Human, plain-spoken values copy — not corporate mush.

### MEDIOCRE
- Homepage section order is CMS-driven, not editorial: *"Highlights and News"* (a webinar + podcast) sits **above** the value proposition.
- Customer names are rendered as a **plain list of text links** rather than a designed proof moment.
- Service descriptions are good but live two clicks deep.
- Imagery is generic enterprise stock (team collaboration, infrastructure).

### BAD
- **The 220 / €31M / 40% / 200 numbers are buried on /about-us.** The strongest credibility signals the company has are absent from the homepage.
- **Script typeface (LFS Adelia) for headings.** In an enterprise-AI context this reads decorative and undercuts authority; it is also weak for legibility at small sizes and in long headings.
- **Navigation overload** — Services expands to 11 links across 3 sub-groups, Technologies to 7, plus Norrin, Contact and 5 language options. High cognitive cost, no prioritisation.
- Nothing on the site **visualises the actual thing they do**. A company whose entire thesis is the pilot→production gap never shows that gap.

### OUTDATED
- Templated HubSpot module rhythm — every section is a full-width band with centered text.
- Cookie-consent placeholders leaking into content (*"You must give consent first to display media."*).
- Flat, uniform vertical spacing; no compositional variety or density contrast.

---

## 4. WHAT SHOULD BE RETHOUGHT

1. **Lead with the thesis, not the newsroom.** "Won in production" is the first thing a visitor should meet.
2. **Promote the proof.** Numbers and customer roster belong high on the page, designed — not as text links.
3. **Replace the script display face** with a typographic system that carries Nordic character *and* enterprise authority.
4. **Collapse the IA.** Four roles, not eleven service links.
5. **Visualise the pilot→production gap** — make the core argument something you can *see*, not just read.
6. **Use the nature palette with discipline** — moss as the ground, sun as the single signal colour.
